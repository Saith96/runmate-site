Why RunMate

Running is better together

Finding someone who runs your distance, at your pace, at your time, usually means a group chat full of maybes. RunMate puts the runs on a map.

Runs near you

Map, list and calendar of upcoming runs. Every run shows where to meet, when, how far and how fast, so you know if it's for you.

A chat for every run

Everyone who joins gets the run's group chat, so last-minute changes reach the right people.

Safe and respectful

Block anyone, report in two taps, messages are filtered, and you can delete your account from the app.

A look inside

Post it, find it, run it

How it works

From idea to running together

  1. Post a run

    Pick the meeting point on the map, the date and time, the distance, pace and difficulty. Repeat it every week if you like.

  2. Others join

    Runners nearby see it on the map, list or calendar and join with one tap. The run's chat opens for everyone in it.

  3. Run, then rate

    Meet up and run. Afterwards, rate the host and see your runs in My Runs.

What's inside

Everything for running with others

  • Map, list and calendar of runs, with “Near Me”
  • Distance, pace, difficulty, tags and a maximum number of runners
  • Repeating runs: daily, weekly, every two weeks or monthly
  • A group chat for every run
  • Guided running tours by local guides
  • Cafés and shops that welcome runners, with their runner discounts

RunMate is free. Guides and businesses use the Pro Zone to list tours and places on the map, and Pro is free while RunMate is new.

Your runs, not your data

RunMate has no ads, no tracking and no analytics. Your location is used on your phone to show runs near you, only when you ask for it.

Questions

Frequently asked

Is RunMate free?

Yes. Posting, finding and joining runs is free, and Pro is free at the moment too.

Why do I need an account?

So other runners know who is hosting and who is coming, and so the run's chat reaches the right people. Sign in with Apple, Google or email.

Is my location shared?

No. RunMate uses your location on your phone to show runs near you, only when you use Near Me or the map. Only the meeting points of runs are shared.

What if someone is abusive?

Block them and report them from their profile or a chat message. Blocking hides their runs, messages and reviews. We review reports, usually within 24 hours.

Can I delete my account?

Yes, in Account → Delete account. It deletes your account and your data.
