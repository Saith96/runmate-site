Privacy Policy

Last updated: 24 November 2026 (version 1).

In short: RunMate needs an account so other runners can see the runs you post and join. We keep what you put in the app (your profile, runs, messages, reviews) on our server, in the European Union, so it can be shown to others. We don't show ads, we don't track you, the app has no analytics, and we never sell your data. In the app you can download a copy of your data and delete your account at any time.

Who is responsible

RunMate is made by Saith Developments ("we"), the data controller for the data described here. Contact for anything about your data: saithdevelopments@gmail.com. We are a very small team, so we have not appointed a data protection officer; write to the address above and we'll answer within one month.

You need to give the account data to use RunMate; everything else is up to you. We don't make automated decisions that have legal or similar effects on you, and we don't build profiles of you.

What others can see

Runs, tours, businesses, your display name, photo and reviews are visible to other RunMate users; who joins a run is visible only to signed-in users. Chat messages are visible only to the people taking part in that run or tour. When you book a tour, the guide sees your name and booking. Reports are visible only to us. Your email address is never shown to other users.

Your location

RunMate asks for your location only when you use "Near Me" or the map. It is used on your phone to centre the map and to show runs close to you; it is not sent to our server or shared with other users, and it is never used in the background. If you choose to, you can use your current position as the meeting point of a run you post; that meeting point is then saved with the run and is public to other RunMate users, like the rest of the run.

Photos

RunMate only reads the photos you pick yourself: your profile picture and any photo you choose to send in a run chat. It has no access to the rest of your photo library.

Your password and security

We never see or store your password in readable form: it is stored only as a salted bcrypt hash by our authentication provider, and new passwords must have at least 8 characters with letters and numbers. If you use Sign in with Apple or Google, we have no password for you at all. Your login session is kept in the iPhone Keychain or, on Android, encrypted with a key held in the phone's secure hardware, and is not included in cloud backups. All data travels encrypted (HTTPS/TLS), the database only lets each user change their own data, and access to the server is limited to us.

Who processes the data for us

  • Supabase (database, sign-in, file storage, sending account emails): our data processor, with your data stored on servers in Frankfurt, Germany (EU).
  • Google (Gmail): sends tour booking confirmation emails from our address, as our processor.
  • Apple and Google, only if you choose Sign in with Apple or Google: they confirm who you are, under their own privacy policies.
  • Maps: to draw the map, your phone downloads map images from Apple Maps (iPhone) or OpenStreetMap (Android) for the area you look at, which reveals that area and your IP address to the map provider; address search uses Apple's or Google's geocoding service on your phone.
  • GitHub (Microsoft) hosts this website and keeps short technical logs of visits.

Some of these companies are based in the United States or may access data from there. Where that happens, the transfer is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or by the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses in their data processing terms. We don't share your data with anyone else, and we don't sell or rent it.

Payments

This version of RunMate takes no payments. Pro is free. If paid guided runs or tours are switched on later, payments will be handled by Stripe, and we will receive only the result of the payment, never your card details. This policy will be updated first.

What we don't do

  • No advertising and no advertising identifiers.
  • No tracking across other companies' apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting software in the app, and no cookies on this website.
  • We don't sell or rent your data to anyone.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and content: until you delete them. Account → Delete account deletes your account, profile, runs, messages, reviews and the rest of your records from our server straight away; photo files you uploaded are removed from storage within 24 hours.
  • Photos sent in a run chat: 30 days after the run (the message stays, with a note that the photo was removed).
  • Run and tour chat messages: 12 months after the run, or after the message for tours.
  • Notifications: 60 days after you read them, 6 months at most.
  • Reports about users and business check-ins: 12 months.
  • Sign-ups never confirmed by email: 30 days.
  • Your acceptance of the terms: as long as your account exists.
  • Server logs: a few days, under our hosting provider's settings.

These deletions run automatically every night.

Your rights

Under the GDPR (and the UK GDPR) you have the right to:

  • access your data and take it with you: Account → Download my data gives you a copy of everything linked to your account as a file;
  • correct it: Account → Edit Profile, or edit your runs;
  • delete it: Account → Delete account, or email us (see Support);
  • restrict or object to processing based on our legitimate interest;
  • withdraw your agreement at any time, by deleting your account.

For anything the app doesn't do, write to saithdevelopments@gmail.com from your account's email address. We answer within one month and it's free. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live or work, or to the Italian Garante per la protezione dei dati personali (garanteprivacy.it).

Children

RunMate is only for people aged 16 and over; everyone confirms this when they first sign in. We don't knowingly collect data from younger children. If you think a child has an account, write to us and we'll delete it.

Changes

If this policy changes we'll update this page and the date above. If the change matters, the app will ask you to read and accept the new version.
